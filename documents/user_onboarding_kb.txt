CATEGORY: LOGIN

ISSUE: Cannot login to my account
QUESTION: I cannot login to my account. What should I do?
ANSWER: If you're unable to login, please follow these steps: 1) Verify you're using the correct email address and password. 2) Check if Caps Lock is enabled. 3) Clear your browser cache and cookies. 4) Try using a different browser or incognito mode. 5) If the issue persists, use the "Forgot Password" option to reset your password. 6) If you still cannot login after trying these steps, contact our support team with your registered email address.

ISSUE: Forgot password
QUESTION: I forgot my password. How can I reset it?
ANSWER: To reset your password: 1) Click on the "Forgot Password" link on the login page. 2) Enter your registered email address. 3) Check your email inbox (and spam folder) for a password reset link. 4) The link is valid for 24 hours. 5) Click the link and create a new password. Your password must be at least 8 characters long and include uppercase letters, lowercase letters, numbers, and special characters. 6) After resetting, login with your new password.

ISSUE: Password reset link not received
QUESTION: I requested a password reset but didn't receive the email. What should I do?
ANSWER: If you haven't received the password reset email: 1) Wait for 10-15 minutes as there might be a delay. 2) Check your spam or junk folder. 3) Verify that you entered the correct email address. 4) Add noreply@yourdomain.com to your safe senders list. 5) Try requesting the reset link again. 6) If you still don't receive it after 30 minutes, contact our support team to verify your account status.

ISSUE: Password reset link expired
QUESTION: The password reset link shows as expired. What should I do?
ANSWER: Password reset links expire after 24 hours for security reasons. To get a new link: 1) Go back to the login page. 2) Click "Forgot Password" again. 3) Enter your email address. 4) You'll receive a new password reset link. 5) Complete the password reset within 24 hours. 6) Make sure your device's system clock is set correctly, as this can affect link validation.

ISSUE: Account locked after failed login attempts
QUESTION: My account is locked after multiple failed login attempts. How do I unlock it?
ANSWER: For security purposes, accounts are temporarily locked after 5 consecutive failed login attempts. You have two options: 1) Wait for 30 minutes and the account will automatically unlock. 2) Use the "Forgot Password" feature to reset your password immediately, which will also unlock your account. 3) If you need urgent access, contact our support team with your account details and they can manually unlock your account after identity verification.

ISSUE: Invalid credentials error
QUESTION: I keep getting "invalid credentials" error even though my password is correct. Why?
ANSWER: If you're certain your credentials are correct but still getting this error: 1) Ensure there are no extra spaces before or after your email or password. 2) Type your password manually instead of copy-pasting. 3) Check if Caps Lock is on. 4) Verify you're logging into the correct platform (not a test or staging environment). 5) Clear your browser cache and cookies. 6) Try a different browser. 7) If the issue persists, reset your password to ensure you have the current correct password.

ISSUE: Session timeout
QUESTION: Why do I keep getting logged out automatically?
ANSWER: Automatic logout occurs due to session timeout for security purposes. Your session expires after 30 minutes of inactivity. To maintain longer sessions: 1) Enable the "Remember Me" option when logging in (extends session to 30 days). 2) Avoid using "Remember Me" on shared or public computers. 3) Check your browser settings to ensure it's not automatically clearing cookies. 4) Disable browser extensions that clear session data. 5) If working on important tasks, save your work regularly. If you need extended session time for business purposes, contact your administrator.

ISSUE: Two-factor authentication not working
QUESTION: My two-factor authentication code is not working. What should I do?
ANSWER: If your 2FA code isn't working: 1) Ensure your device time is synchronized correctly (required for time-based codes). 2) Use the most recent code, as they expire every 30-60 seconds. 3) Wait for a new code to generate if the current one is almost expired. 4) If using SMS, wait 2-3 minutes for delivery. 5) Try using backup codes provided during 2FA setup. 6) Check if the code is being sent to the correct phone number or email. 7) If you've lost access to your authentication method, contact support to temporarily disable 2FA after identity verification.

ISSUE: Cannot login after email change
QUESTION: I changed my email address and now cannot login. How do I fix this?
ANSWER: If your email was changed at the company level: 1) Try logging in with your old email address first. 2) If that doesn't work, contact your IT administrator or HR department. 3) They can update your email in the system. 4) Alternatively, contact our support team with both your old and new email addresses, along with verification details like employee ID or previous order numbers. 5) Once updated, you'll receive a confirmation email at your new address. 6) All previous sessions will be logged out for security. 7) Update your email in profile settings after regaining access.

ISSUE: Login from new device or location
QUESTION: I'm trying to login from a new device/location and it's being blocked. What should I do?
ANSWER: For security, logins from new devices or unusual locations may require additional verification: 1) Check your email for a security verification message. 2) Click the verification link or enter the provided code. 3) If you didn't receive the email, check your spam folder. 4) You may be asked to answer security questions. 5) Once verified, you can mark the device as trusted to avoid future verifications. 6) If you suspect unauthorized access attempts, immediately change your password and enable 2FA. 7) Contact support if legitimate access is being blocked.

CATEGORY: OTP (ONE-TIME PASSWORD)

ISSUE: OTP not received
QUESTION: I'm not receiving the OTP code. What should I do?
ANSWER: If you're not receiving OTP codes: 1) Wait 2-3 minutes as SMS/email delivery can be delayed. 2) Check your spam or junk folder for email OTPs. 3) Ensure your phone has good signal strength for SMS. 4) Verify the phone number/email in your profile is correct. 5) Try clicking "Resend OTP" (wait at least 60 seconds before resending). 6) Check if your phone's message inbox is full. 7) Add our sender number/email to your contacts. 8) Disable any SMS blocking or spam filtering apps temporarily. 9) Try receiving OTP via alternative method (email instead of SMS, or vice versa). 10) If still not receiving, contact support to verify your contact information and manually verify your action.

ISSUE: OTP expired or invalid
QUESTION: The OTP code I entered shows as expired or invalid. Why?
ANSWER: OTP codes have security measures that can cause this: 1) OTP codes expire after 10 minutes for security. Request a new code if expired. 2) Ensure you're entering the most recent code if you requested multiple. 3) Type the code carefully - no spaces, all digits. 4) Codes are case-sensitive if alphanumeric. 5) Don't copy-paste as it may include extra characters. 6) Some codes are single-use only and expire after one attempt. 7) Wait 60 seconds before requesting a new code. 8) Clear browser cache if the issue persists. 9) Ensure your device's time is synchronized correctly. 10) If you've tried multiple codes, your account may be temporarily locked for security - wait 15 minutes or contact support.

ISSUE: How long is OTP valid
QUESTION: How long do I have to enter the OTP code before it expires?
ANSWER: OTP code validity: 1) Standard validity: 10 minutes from generation. 2) After 10 minutes, the code becomes invalid and you must request a new one. 3) Codes are single-use - once used successfully, they cannot be reused. 4) If you request a new code, the previous code becomes invalid immediately. 5) For security reasons, this cannot be extended. 6) Make sure you're ready to complete the action before requesting the OTP. 7) Have the verification page open and ready when OTP arrives. 8) If you know you'll need more time, wait to request the OTP until you're ready. 9) Failed attempts don't extend the validity period. 10) After 5 failed attempts, you may need to wait 15 minutes before requesting a new code.

ISSUE: OTP for different actions
QUESTION: When do I need to enter an OTP code?
ANSWER: OTP codes are required for these security-sensitive actions: 1) Login from new device or unusual location. 2) Changing email address. 3) Changing phone number. 4) Updating payment methods or billing information. 5) Large financial transactions. 6) Withdrawals or refunds. 7) Changing password (some cases). 8) Disabling two-factor authentication. 9) Deleting account. 10) Adding new users to business accounts. 11) Accessing sensitive personal information. 12) API key generation or reset. This protects your account from unauthorized access. You can reduce OTP requests by marking devices as trusted and enabling persistent login on secure devices.

ISSUE: OTP sent to wrong number/email
QUESTION: The OTP is being sent to a phone number/email I no longer have access to. What can I do?
ANSWER: If OTP is sent to inaccessible contact information: 1) Try logging in with alternative methods if available (social login, backup email). 2) Click "Use different method" or "Send to backup email/phone" if available. 3) Contact support immediately with: Your account details, Proof of identity, New contact information you have access to. 4) Support will verify your identity through security questions or documents. 5) They can temporarily disable OTP or update contact information manually. 6) Once access is restored, immediately update your profile with current contact details. 7) Set up backup authentication methods. 8) Save backup codes in a secure location. 9) Keep your profile contact information current to avoid this issue. 10) Consider using authenticator apps instead of SMS for 2FA as they're not tied to a phone number.

ISSUE: Multiple OTP requests
QUESTION: Can I request multiple OTP codes? What happens to the old ones?
ANSWER: Regarding multiple OTP requests: 1) Yes, you can request new OTP codes if needed. 2) Each new OTP code invalidates the previous one immediately. 3) Only the most recent code will work. 4) Wait at least 60 seconds between requests to avoid rate limiting. 5) You can request up to 5 OTP codes within 15 minutes. 6) After 5 attempts, you'll be temporarily blocked for 15 minutes. 7) This prevents automated attacks on your account. 8) If you're not receiving codes, requesting more won't help - check your contact method instead. 9) Each OTP request sends a new notification. 10) If you're repeatedly requesting codes due to entry errors, double-check you're entering them correctly before requesting new ones.

ISSUE: OTP for transaction verification
QUESTION: Why do I need to enter an OTP to complete my transaction?
ANSWER: OTP for transactions is a security measure: 1) Protects against unauthorized transactions on your account. 2) Required for transactions above certain amounts (varies by account type). 3) Verifies that you're authorizing the transaction in real-time. 4) Prevents fraudulent charges if your account is compromised. 5) Required by payment regulations in many regions. 6) The OTP is sent to your registered phone/email. 7) You have 10 minutes to enter the code and complete the transaction. 8) If OTP is not entered, the transaction will be cancelled. 9) You'll need to restart the transaction and request a new OTP. 10) Trusted devices may have reduced OTP requirements after initial setup. This extra step ensures your account and funds are secure.

ISSUE: Authenticator app vs SMS OTP
QUESTION: What's the difference between authenticator app and SMS OTP? Which is better?
ANSWER: Comparison of OTP methods: **Authenticator App (Recommended):** 1) Works offline - generates codes without internet or cell service. 2) More secure - not vulnerable to SIM swapping attacks. 3) Faster - instant code generation. 4) Free with no SMS charges. 5) Apps like Google Authenticator, Authy, Microsoft Authenticator. 6) Codes rotate every 30 seconds. 7) Can backup to cloud (Authy) or manually save QR code. **SMS OTP:** 1) Convenient - no additional app needed. 2) Accessible on any phone. 3) May have delivery delays. 4) Vulnerable to SIM swapping and interception. 5) May incur SMS charges when roaming. 6) Dependent on cellular coverage. **Recommendation:** Use authenticator app for primary 2FA, keep SMS as backup method. Save backup codes in a secure password manager. Enable multiple 2FA methods for redundancy.

ISSUE: Lost access to OTP method
QUESTION: I lost my phone/access to my authenticator app. How do I login now?
ANSWER: If you've lost access to your OTP method: 1) Use backup codes if you saved them during 2FA setup - each code is single-use. 2) Try alternative OTP delivery method (if you set up backup email/phone). 3) Click "Use backup method" or "Can't access your code?" on the login page. 4) Contact support with: Account username/email, Recent transaction details, Security question answers, Government-issued ID for verification. 5) Support will verify your identity and temporarily disable 2FA. 6) You'll receive a secure temporary access code via email. 7) Login and immediately set up new 2FA method. 8) Generate and save new backup codes. 9) For authenticator apps: If you backed up to cloud (Authy), restore on new device. If using Google Authenticator without backup, you'll need to reconfigure. 10) Prevention: Always save backup codes, set up multiple 2FA methods, back up authenticator app if possible.

ISSUE: OTP rate limiting
QUESTION: I'm getting an error saying I've requested too many OTP codes. What does this mean?
ANSWER: OTP rate limiting is a security feature: 1) You can request maximum 5 OTP codes within 15 minutes. 2) After 5 attempts, you're temporarily blocked for security. 3) Waiting period: 15-30 minutes depending on security settings. 4) This prevents brute force attacks and spam. 5) The countdown timer shows when you can try again. 6) While waiting, do NOT continue requesting codes as it extends the block. 7) Use this time to verify your contact information is correct. 8) Clear browser cache and try from incognito mode. 9) Ensure you're entering OTPs correctly to avoid wasting attempts. 10) If you're frequently hitting rate limits, contact support to investigate why codes aren't working. After the waiting period, you'll be able to request OTP again.

What you can do: 1) Critical notifications (security, transactions) are prioritized and sent immediately. 2) Add our domain to safe senders to improve delivery speed. 3) Use in-app notifications for real-time updates. 4) Enable push notifications for instant alerts. 5) Check notification timestamp vs received time to identify delays. If delays exceed 1 hour consistently, contact support.

CATEGORY: ACCOUNT SECURITY

ISSUE: Suspicious account activity
QUESTION: I see login activity or actions I didn't perform. What should I do?
ANSWER: If you notice suspicious activity: **Immediate Actions:** 1) Change your password immediately. 2) Go to Settings > Security > Active Sessions and end all sessions. 3) Review recent account activity/login history. 4) Enable two-factor authentication if not already enabled. 5) Check for unauthorized email or phone number changes. 6) Review any financial transactions or purchases made. **Investigation Steps:** 1) Check "Login History" for unfamiliar locations or devices. 2) Look for emails about password changes you didn't make. 3) Check if any personal information was modified. 4) Review connected apps and revoke suspicious access. 5) Check for forwarding rules in email settings. **Contact Support:** 1) Report the suspicious activity immediately. 2) Provide details: dates, times, types of activity. 3) Support will investigate and secure your account. 4) You may need to verify your identity. 5) Support can help identify if activity was actually yours. **Prevention:** 1) Use strong, unique passwords. 2) Never share your password. 3) Don't click suspicious email links. 4) Keep 2FA enabled. 5) Review security regularly.

ISSUE: Account security best practices
QUESTION: What are the best practices to keep my account secure?
ANSWER: Follow these security best practices: **Password Security:** 1) Use a strong password: minimum 12 characters, mix of uppercase, lowercase, numbers, special characters. 2) Never reuse passwords across different sites. 3) Use a password manager to generate and store passwords. 4) Change password if you suspect it's compromised. 5) Don't share your password with anyone. **Two-Factor Authentication:** 1) Enable 2FA using authenticator app (most secure). 2) Save backup codes in a secure location. 3) Set up multiple 2FA methods for redundancy. **Account Monitoring:** 1) Review login activity regularly. 2) Check active sessions monthly and end unknown sessions. 3) Monitor notification emails for unauthorized changes. 4) Set up alerts for security events. **General Security:** 1) Keep email account secure (it's your recovery method). 2) Be cautious of phishing emails - verify sender before clicking links. 3) Don't login on public/shared computers. 4) Use secure, private networks (avoid public WiFi for sensitive actions). 5) Keep devices and browsers updated. 6) Revoke access for apps you no longer use. 7) Don't save passwords on shared devices. Review security settings quarterly.

ISSUE: Secure account recovery
QUESTION: How can I ensure I can recover my account if locked out?
ANSWER: Set up these recovery options: **Primary Recovery Methods:** 1) Verified email address - keep current and accessible. 2) Verified phone number - keep active. 3) Backup email address - add a secondary email. 4) Security questions - set up with memorable answers (don't use easily guessable info). **Two-Factor Authentication Backup:** 1) Save backup codes when setting up 2FA. 2) Print and store codes securely offline. 3) Set up multiple 2FA methods (authenticator + SMS). 4) Back up authenticator app if supported (Authy allows cloud backup). **Additional Security:** 1) Keep profile information current. 2) Add trusted devices for faster recovery. 3) Set up trusted contacts (some platforms offer this). 4) Keep identification documents handy for identity verification. **Recovery Information Storage:** 1) Use a password manager for backup codes. 2) Store recovery info separate from password. 3) Keep physical copy in secure location. 4) Don't share recovery information with others. **Test Recovery Process:** 1) Periodically verify recovery email/phone works. 2) Ensure you remember security question answers. 3) Keep backup codes accessible. Regular review ensures smooth recovery if needed.
