CATEGORY: PAYMENT FAILURE

ISSUE: Payment declined or failed
QUESTION: My payment was declined. Why did this happen and what should I do?
ANSWER: Payment declines occur for several reasons: **Common Causes:** 1) Insufficient funds in account. 2) Card has expired - check expiry date. 3) Incorrect card details (number, CVV, expiry). 4) Billing address doesn't match card on file. 5) Card blocked for online/international transactions. 6) Bank fraud protection triggered. 7) Daily transaction limit exceeded. 8) Card not supported (we accept Visa, Mastercard, Amex, Discover). **What to Do:** 1) Verify all card details are entered correctly. 2) Check with your bank if the card is blocked. 3) Ensure sufficient funds or credit available. 4) Try a different payment method. 5) Contact your bank to authorize the transaction. 6) Clear browser cache and try again. 7) Wait 10 minutes and retry if temporarily declined. 8) Use a different card if issues persist. **Still Having Issues?** Contact support with: Transaction reference number, Last 4 digits of card used, Error message received. We'll help investigate and process your payment.

ISSUE: Card keeps getting declined
QUESTION: I've tried multiple times but my card keeps getting declined. What's wrong?
ANSWER: If your card is repeatedly declined: **Verification Steps:** 1) Double-check card number is entered correctly (no spaces or dashes). 2) Verify CVV is the 3-4 digit code on back/front of card. 3) Confirm expiry date is in MM/YY format and card hasn't expired. 4) Ensure billing address exactly matches your bank records. 5) Check that name on card matches name entered. **Contact Your Bank:** 1) Call the number on back of your card. 2) Ask if there are any blocks or restrictions. 3) Inform them you're trying to make a legitimate purchase. 4) Ask them to authorize our merchant name. 5) Confirm the card is activated for online purchases. 6) Check if international transactions are enabled (if applicable). **Alternative Solutions:** 1) Try a different card if available. 2) Use PayPal or alternative payment method. 3) Ask your bank about pre-authorized payments. 4) Check if you've reached daily transaction limits. 5) Wait 24 hours as temporary blocks may lift automatically. **Avoid Multiple Attempts:** Too many failed attempts can trigger additional security blocks. After 3 failed attempts, contact your bank before trying again. If verified everything is correct and card still declines, contact support for manual payment processing.

ISSUE: Payment processing but not completed
QUESTION: My payment has been processing for a long time. Is something wrong?
ANSWER: Payment processing times vary: **Normal Processing Times:** 1) Credit/Debit cards: 30 seconds to 2 minutes. 2) PayPal: 1-2 minutes. 3) Bank transfer: 1-3 business days. 4) Digital wallets: 1-5 minutes. **If Payment is Stuck:** 1) Don't refresh the page or click back during processing. 2) Wait at least 10 minutes before taking action. 3) Check your email for payment confirmation. 4) Check bank/card statement for pending charge. 5) Go to Account > Billing > Transaction History to check status. **Payment Status Meanings:** 1) Processing: Payment is being verified by bank. 2) Pending: Awaiting bank confirmation (may take up to 24 hours). 3) Completed: Payment successful and confirmed. 4) Failed: Payment was declined - you won't be charged. **What to Do:** 1) If status shows "Pending" after 24 hours, contact support. 2) If status shows "Processing" after 10 minutes, check with your bank. 3) Don't attempt duplicate payment until confirming first one failed. 4) Check if you received a transaction ID - this confirms payment was submitted. 5) Contact support with transaction ID if unsure. **To Avoid Issues:** Don't close browser or navigate away until you see confirmation page. Keep internet connection stable during payment. Check confirmation email before assuming failure.

ISSUE: Duplicate charges
QUESTION: I was charged multiple times for a single purchase. How do I get a refund?
ANSWER: Duplicate charges can occur due to technical issues: **Why This Happens:** 1) Clicking payment button multiple times. 2) Browser refresh during payment processing. 3) Network timeout causing retry. 4) System error during processing. **Check Your Statement:** 1) Verify these are actual charges, not just authorization holds. 2) Authorization holds appear like charges but aren't finalized - they drop off in 3-5 days. 3) Pending transactions may show multiple times but only one will complete. 4) Check if charges have posted or are still pending. **Immediate Steps:** 1) Go to Account > Billing > Transaction History. 2) Note all transaction IDs and amounts. 3) Check if multiple subscriptions or orders were created. 4) Take screenshots of duplicate charges on your statement. **Getting Refund:** 1) Contact support immediately with: All transaction IDs, Bank statement showing duplicates, Date and time of payment, Amount charged. 2) We'll investigate within 24 hours. 3) Confirmed duplicate charges are refunded within 5-7 business days. 4) Refund goes to original payment method. 5) You'll receive email confirmation once refund is processed. **Prevention:** Only click "Pay" button once. Don't refresh page during payment. Wait for confirmation page before navigating away. If you receive error message, check account first before retrying payment.

ISSUE: Payment gateway error
QUESTION: I'm getting a payment gateway error. What does this mean?
ANSWER: Payment gateway errors indicate connection issues: **Common Error Types:** 1) "Gateway Timeout" - Connection between our system and payment processor failed. 2) "Gateway Unavailable" - Payment processor is temporarily down. 3) "Invalid Gateway Response" - Technical communication error. 4) "Gateway Error 500" - Internal payment processor error. **What These Mean:** These are temporary technical issues, not problems with your payment method. Your card/account is not charged when these errors occur. **Solutions:** 1) Wait 5-10 minutes and try again. 2) Clear browser cache and cookies. 3) Try a different browser or device. 4) Disable VPN if you're using one. 5) Check our status page for known payment issues. 6) Try a different payment method if available. 7) Use incognito/private browsing mode. **If Error Persists:** 1) Check back in 30 minutes - usually resolved quickly. 2) Try during off-peak hours (early morning or late evening). 3) Contact support to check if there's scheduled maintenance. 4) We can process payment manually through support if urgent. 5) Sign up for status notifications to be alerted when resolved. **Checking Status:** Visit status.yourdomain.com for real-time payment gateway status. Follow our social media for maintenance announcements. Gateway issues are typically resolved within 1-2 hours.

ISSUE: 3D Secure verification
QUESTION: What is 3D Secure and why am I being asked to verify my payment?
ANSWER: 3D Secure is an additional security layer for online card payments: **What It Is:** 1) Security protocol required by banks (Visa Secure, Mastercard ID Check). 2) Adds extra authentication to verify you're the cardholder. 3) Required by regulations in many countries (especially Europe). 4) Protects against unauthorized card use. **How It Works:** 1) After entering card details, you're redirected to your bank's page. 2) You verify identity through: SMS code to registered phone, Bank app authentication, Password or PIN, Biometric verification (fingerprint/face ID). 3) After verification, you're returned to complete purchase. 4) Process takes 30-60 seconds. **Common Issues:** 1) Not receiving verification code - check phone number is current with your bank. 2) Verification page not loading - disable popup blockers. 3) Timeout during verification - complete within 5 minutes. 4) Wrong password - use your banking password/PIN, not our site password. **Troubleshooting:** 1) Ensure popups are allowed for our site. 2) Don't close the verification window. 3) Check that you're using updated browser. 4) Contact your bank if you can't complete verification. 5) Try different payment method if issues persist. **Security:** This verification proves you're the legitimate cardholder. Never share verification codes with anyone. Your bank will never call asking for these codes. We don't see your banking credentials during this process.

QUESTION: I'm trying to save my payment method for future use but it's not saving. Why?
ANSWER: If payment methods aren't being saved: **Common Causes:** 1) Browser privacy settings blocking cookies. 2) Using guest checkout mode. 3) Account type doesn't allow saved cards. 4) Card information validation failed. 5) Browser autofill interfering with form. **Solutions:** 1) Ensure you're logged into your account (guest checkouts don't save). 2) Check the "Save for future use" or "Remember this card" checkbox. 3) Complete all required fields including billing address. 4) Allow cookies for our website in browser settings. 5) Disable autofill and type card information manually. 6) Try a different browser if issues persist. 7) Verify CVV is correct (we don't store CVV for security). **After Adding Card:** 1) Go to Profile Settings > Payment Methods to verify it's saved. 2) You should see last 4 digits of card and card type. 3) Set as default payment method if desired. 4) Verify billing address is complete. **Security Notes:** 1) We store card information securely using tokenization. 2) Full card number is never displayed after saving (only last 4 digits). 3) CVV is never stored - must be entered each time for security. 4) You can remove saved cards anytime. 5) Cards are automatically removed if they expire. **Multiple Cards:** You can save multiple payment methods. Each method needs unique billing information. Default payment method is used for automatic renewals.

ISSUE: Authorization hold vs actual charge
QUESTION: I see a charge on my card but my account shows payment is pending. What's happening?
ANSWER: Understanding authorization holds vs charges: **Authorization Hold:** 1) Temporary hold placed when you initiate payment. 2) Verifies funds are available but doesn't transfer them yet. 3) Shows as "pending" on your bank statement. 4) May appear as duplicate transactions (both hold and charge). 5) Holds drop off in 3-5 business days if not captured. **Actual Charge:** 1) Funds are actually transferred to us. 2) Shows as "posted" or "completed" on statement. 3) Only happens after we confirm your order/service. **Timeline:** 1) Authorization: Immediate when you click "Pay". 2) Hold period: 1-24 hours while we process. 3) Capture: Once order is confirmed. 4) Hold release: 3-5 days after capture (for the hold amount). **Why You See Both:** 1) Bank shows authorization and capture separately initially. 2) Authorization will disappear, leaving only actual charge. 3) Total charged is only the actual purchase amount, not both. **What to Do:** 1) Wait 3-5 business days for holds to clear. 2) Check Account > Orders to see payment status. 3) Look for "Posted" vs "Pending" on your statement. 4) Don't dispute the authorization - it will clear automatically. 5) Contact support if both transactions post as actual charges (this would be duplicate billing). **For Refunds:** Authorization holds: Cancel immediately if order is cancelled before processing. Posted charges: Take 5-10 business days to refund after request.

ISSUE: Chargeback vs refund request
QUESTION: What's the difference between requesting a refund and filing a chargeback? Which should I do?
ANSWER: Understanding refunds vs chargebacks: **Refund Request (Direct with Us):** **Process:** 1) You contact us directly. 2) We review and approve/deny. 3) Money returned within 7-14 days. 4) Handled through our customer service. **Advantages:** 1) Faster resolution (days vs months). 2) Maintains good account standing. 3) More flexible resolution options. 4) No bank fees or complications. 5) Can still use service during resolution. **Chargeback (Through Bank):** **Process:** 1) You contact bank/card issuer. 2) Bank disputes charge on your behalf. 3) We must respond with evidence. 4) Bank makes final decision. 5) Can take 60-90 days. **When Banks Accept Chargebacks:** 1) Unauthorized transaction (fraud). 2) Service not provided as described. 3) Charged incorrect amount. 4) Duplicate billing. 5) Merchant refused legitimate refund request. **Consequences of Chargebacks:** 1) Account may be immediately suspended. 2) Access to services blocked during investigation. 3) Chargeback fee ($15-25) charged to merchant. 4) Too many chargebacks can result in permanent ban. 5) Affects merchant's ability to process payments. **Our Recommendation:** **Try Refund Request First:** 1) Contact support with issue. 2) Give us 24-48 hours to respond. 3) We resolve 95% of payment issues directly. 4) Faster, simpler, maintains account access. **File Chargeback If:** 1) We don't respond to refund requests. 2) Clear fraud or unauthorized charge. 3) We refused legitimate refund without valid reason. 4) You tried direct resolution unsuccessfully. **Best Practice:** Document all communication with us before filing chargeback. Banks want to see you attempted direct resolution first. Most payment issues are misunderstandings or technical errors easily resolved directly.

ANSWER: When automatic renewal payments fail: **What Happens Immediately:** 1) You receive email notification of failed payment. 2) We automatically retry payment within 24 hours. 3) Your access continues during grace period. 4) Second retry after 3 days if first retry fails. 5) Final retry after 7 days. **Grace Period:** 1) Account remains active for 7-14 days (depends on plan). 2) You can still use services during this time. 3) Grace period allows time to fix payment issue. 4) Reminder emails sent every 2-3 days. **Why Payment Failed:** 1) Insufficient funds in account. 2) Card expired. 3) Card cancelled or blocked. 4) Billing address changed. 5) Bank declined recurring payment. 6) Credit limit reached. **How to Fix:** **Update Payment Method:** 1) Go to Account Settings > Billing > Payment Methods. 2) Update existing card details (especially expiry date). 3) Or add new payment method. 4) Set as default for subscriptions. 5) Click "Retry Payment Now" button. **Manual Payment:** 1) Go to Billing > Pending Invoices. 2) Find the failed payment. 3) Click "Pay Now". 4) Enter payment details and complete. **If Grace Period Expires:** 1) Account is suspended (not deleted). 2) Access to services blocked. 3) Data retained for 30 days. 4) Can reactivate by paying outstanding amount. 5) After 30 days, account may be deleted (data loss). **To Reactivate Suspended Account:** 1) Login (you can still access account management). 2) Go to Billing > Outstanding Balance. 3) Click "Pay and Reactivate". 4) Complete payment. 5) Access restored within 1 hour. 6) No data loss if reactivated within 30 days. **Prevention:** 1) Keep payment method updated. 2) Ensure card won't expire during subscription period. 3) Enable payment method update notifications. 4) Set calendar reminder to check payment info quarterly. 5) Add backup payment method. 6) Keep sufficient balance for recurring charges. **Contact Support If:** Payment method is valid but still failing. Need payment extension due to temporary financial difficulty. Want to discuss payment plan options. Having technical issues updating payment information.


CATEGORY: SUBSCRIPTIONS & RECURRING PAYMENTS

ISSUE: How subscriptions and recurring payments work
QUESTION: How do subscription payments work? When will I be charged?
ANSWER: Understanding subscription billing: **Initial Charge:** 1) Charged immediately when you subscribe. 2) Amount includes: First billing period (month or year), Any setup fees, Pro-rated amount if starting mid-cycle. 3) You receive confirmation email with next billing date. **Recurring Charges:** **Monthly Subscriptions:** 1) Charged automatically every month on same date. 2) Example: Subscribe on January 15th, Next charge on February 15th, then March 15th, etc. 3) Months with fewer days: Charged on last day (February 28/29 if subscribed on 31st). **Annual Subscriptions:** 1) Charged once per year on anniversary date. 2) Example: Subscribe January 15, 2024, Next charge January 15, 2025. **Billing Notifications:** 1) Reminder email 7 days before renewal. 2) Confirmation email after successful charge. 3) Failed payment notification if charge doesn't go through. **What You're Paying For:** 1) Upcoming billing period (paid in advance). 2) Not paying for past usage. 3) Example: January 15th charge is for January 15 - February 14 service. **Auto-renewal:** 1) Subscriptions automatically renew unless cancelled. 2) No action needed to continue service. 3) Cancel anytime before next billing date. 4) Access continues until end of paid period after cancellation. **Price Changes:** 1) Notified 30 days before any price increases. 2) Can cancel before increase takes effect. 3) Existing subscribers may have grandfathered pricing. **Payment Method:** 1) Uses default payment method on file. 2) Update anytime in Account Settings. 3) Backup method charged if primary fails. **Managing Subscription:** View next billing date: Account > Subscription. Update payment method: Billing > Payment Methods. Cancel renewal: Subscription > Cancel (access continues until period ends). Change plan: Subscription > Change Plan (pro-rated billing).

ISSUE: How to cancel subscription
QUESTION: How do I cancel my subscription? Will I lose access immediately?
ANSWER: Cancelling your subscription: **Cancellation Process:** 1) Login to your account. 2) Go to Account Settings > Subscription. 3) Click "Cancel Subscription" or "Manage Plan". 4) Select cancellation reason (helps us improve). 5) Choose: "Cancel at period end" (recommended - keep access), or "Cancel immediately" (immediate termination). 6) Confirm cancellation. 7) You'll receive confirmation email. **What Happens to Access:** **Cancel at Period End (Recommended):** 1) No refund, but access continues until end of paid period. 2) Example: Paid until January 31st, Cancel on January 10th, Keep access until January 31st. 3) No additional charges. 4) Can reactivate anytime before period ends. **Cancel Immediately:** 1) Access terminates within 24 hours. 2) May qualify for pro-rated refund (check refund policy). 3) Refund request must be submitted separately. 4) Data retention for 30 days. **After Cancellation:** 1) No future charges to your payment method. 2) Access ends at period end (or immediately if selected). 3) Data retained for 30 days after access ends. 4) Download/export your data before cancellation if needed. 5) Confirmation and access end date sent via email. **Reactivating Cancelled Subscription:** Before access ends: 1) Go to Subscription settings. 2) Click "Reactivate" or "Undo Cancellation". 3) No charge until original renewal date. After access ends: 1) Need to start new subscription. 2) Charged immediately for new period. 3) May lose previous pricing if it was promotional. **Common Issues:** **Can't find cancel button:** Some accounts require contacting support to cancel (usually promotional or special plans). **Cancellation not processing:** Clear cache, try different browser, or contact support. **Want to pause instead of cancel:** Some plans offer pause/freeze option (check Subscription settings). **Downgrade instead of cancel:** Consider switching to free plan or lower tier. **Important Notes:** 1) Cancellation doesn't automatically request refund. 2) Cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid next charge. 3) Annual subscriptions can be cancelled anytime but refund may be pro-rated. 4) Free trials can be cancelled during trial period for no charge. If you're cancelling due to issue with service, contact support first - we may be able to help resolve the problem.

billing cycle: Lower price of new plan. 4) Confirmation email with effective date. **Important Considerations:** **When Upgrading:** 1) Check if data/usage fits within new limits. 2) New features remains accessible. 2) Downgrading: Data within new limits remains accessible, excess data may be archived or deleted (notified in advance). **Plan Change Limits:** 1) Can change plans once per billing period. 2) Exception: Can upgrade anytime, multiple times. 3) Downgrading has cooldown period (usually 30 days). **Special Promotions:** 1) Promotional pricing may be lost when changing plans. period. 2) Monthly to Annual: Charged pro-rated annual amount immediately. 3) Usually saves money to switch to annual.
